## The catch-up math

Four seasons, 32 episodes, roughly 48 minutes each. That works out to a little over 25 hours of television, which is a long weekend if you commit or a comfortable two weeks at an episode a night. The [binge calculator](/tools/how-long-to-watch/reacher/) has the full per-season breakdown if you want to plan around a season at a time.

## What to know going in

- Each season is a self-contained case. You can technically start anywhere, but the show rewards watching in order because the recurring faces mean more the second time they turn up.
- The books the seasons are adapted from are standalone too, so there is no wrong entry point on the page either.
- Episodes are dense. Skipping one to "get to the good part" tends to cost you a plot thread you'll need later.
